How this works

This dashboard is a governance oversight layer for AML controls, not transaction monitoring. Use Scenario Simulation to autoplay a control decay or remediation story across several days. The Bank/Regulator toggle changes who can see internal actions. Each card shows the obligation, owner, required level, current level, evidence, risk, status, escalation stage, and audit history.
